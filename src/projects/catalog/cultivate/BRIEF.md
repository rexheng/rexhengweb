# Cultivate — sprite brief

**Project:** Cultivate (tryattend) — event networking capture. Built at Pop the Bubble, London, June 2026.
**Card on cv/index.html:** none this round (playground only).
**Sprite role:** symbolic prop. A QR plate plus a ring of meeting-graph nodes.

## Why this sprite

The GitHub description is the source of truth: QR scan, enrichment, and a graph of who you met. The plate reads as a scan token; the four spheres read as people on a graph.

## Silhouette targets

1. Dark square plate ~1.4×D with a 3×3 QR-like cubie grid (centre cell open).
2. Four emissive teal nodes on a ring around the plate.

## Ability

`pulse` — "Graph!" Nodes radiate from the sprite for ~2s.
