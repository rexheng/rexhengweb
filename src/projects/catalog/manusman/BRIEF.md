# Manusman — sprite brief

**Project:** Manusman — Most Commercial Product, Manus Vibecoding Hackathon 2026 (1st of 60).
**Card on cv/index.html:** existing CV card left unchanged this round.
**Sprite role:** symbolic prop. Stacked briefing cards plus a live-call overlay, reading as meeting prep rather than a mascot.

## Why this sprite

Manusman is a client-meeting prep tool: research lands as a briefing, then an overlay carries it into the call. The silhouette is the product shape (cards + PiP), not a character.

## Silhouette targets

1. Three cream briefing cards, slightly fanned, ~1.4×D × 1.8×D.
2. Dark overlay slab in front with a thin amber status bar.

## Ability

`launch` — "Brief!" One-shot upward impulse, same as Arrow.
